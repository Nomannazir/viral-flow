What makes them work
  • ASMR-style sound design — scissors, glue, tearing paper — triggers dopamine without needing voice

  • Hooks use visual curiosity — viewer can't look away because they want to see the result

04
Managing Multiple Accounts
The portfolio approach
The golden rule: 1 account = 1 niche = 1 voice
Never mix niches on one account. Instagram's algorithm learns what audience to show you to. Cross-niche posting confuses it and tanks reach by 40–60%.
Separate Instagram business accounts. Each niche = new Gmail + new Instagram. Use Meta Business Suite to manage all from one dashboard.
Different pipeline config per account. Clone your n8n workflow, swap the niche filter, topic keywords, voice ID, and affiliate links. 20-minute setup per new account.
Consistent visual identity per account. Same font, same colour scheme, same music style every video. Viewers should recognise the account from the first frame.
